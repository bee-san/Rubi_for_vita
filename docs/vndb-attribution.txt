Rubi converts the combined PSP + Vita Yomitan dictionary into its disk-backed
format 2 with tools/prepare_vndb.py and tools/convert_dictionary.py. This adapted
database remains under ODbL 1.0. The unchanged machine-readable source is bundled
in the source repository at third_party/vndb-names/VNDB-Names-PSP-PSVita.zip and
is available from the source commit's dist/VNDB-Names-PSP-PSVita.zip. Exact input
and output checksums and conversion counts are in docs/vndb-names.json.

The source has 30,713 searchable name rows for 12,151 characters across 1,202
qualifying VNs; name parts of one or two kana are not rows. Rubi retains 30,709
rows: four names exceed its 32-character lookup
limit. One definition exceeds the 7,000-byte text limit and is clipped with an
ellipsis. Shared names retain their source's combined character/game definitions.
No images, biographies, guessed kanji readings or frequency ranks are added.
Aliases may reveal names: VNDB does not give aliases individual spoiler flags.
