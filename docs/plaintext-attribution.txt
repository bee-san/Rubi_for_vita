Bee's Ultimate Dictionary (plaintext.vjdict), built once and frozen
(bee-san/plaintext-dicts, release ultimate-v1; exact snapshots, hashes and
counts: plaintext-dictionaries.json). No automatic dictionary updates are
performed.

Each entry's text is its definition, or a grammar point's English summary,
then one line per kanji of its spelling ("木=tree, wood"). Definitions come
from Daijirin4 -> Sankoku8 -> JMdict, in that priority: deduplication selects
the first source for each spelling and normalized reading, preserving all of
that source's senses and original inflection rules. Source definitions were
supplied as plaintext English conversions. The entries name no source:
attribution is here and in the metadata only. Definitions are capped at
7,000 UTF-8 bytes; clipped ones end in "…". Every source keeps its own
ownership and licence; the plugin software license does not relicense
dictionary content.
